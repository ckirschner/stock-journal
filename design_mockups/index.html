Ledger — three directions

Same journal, same figures, same rules in all three. What differs is where the teaching lives. Invented companies throughout. The written comparison is in NOTES.md beside these files.

A · The ruled page a-ruled-page.html
One column, set like a book. Every explanation unfolds in place under its own line, and folds away. Click any row of the evidence table; click the CALDR row to open the detail page.
 B · Instrument and margin b-instrument-margin.html
Two surfaces: a terse answer plane that never grows a paragraph, and a study margin where every gloss lands. Clicking a list row previews it in the margin; buying and selling happen there too, with today's verdict on top.
 B · Daylight skin b-daylight.html
The chosen direction, reskinned: bright white ground, cool hairlines, Schibsted Grotesk with mono numerals, rounded cards and pill actions. Same structure and behaviour as B; only the look changed.
 B · Nightdesk — the chosen one b-nightdesk.html
The picked direction and skin: Instrument Sans + Red Hat Mono, luminous state colours on deep neutral. One palette, dark, committed — and carrying the owner's six corrections: split header counts, commit in its own bright colour (never the pass-green), rows citing the resolved decision, all four kinds of absence shown, typed° made legible.
 C · The depth dial c-depth-dial.html
One control moves the whole page between three altitudes — Reading, Evidence, Study (keys 1 · 2 · 3). The anxious visit and the study visit are different pages, chosen once per visit, not per element.

Shortcuts for review: a-ruled-page.html#detail opens CALDR directly; b-instrument-margin.html#detail likewise (#detail-m=m-ptb lands with the price-to-book gloss open); c-depth-dial.html#detail-1, #detail-2, #detail-3 open CALDR at each altitude.
